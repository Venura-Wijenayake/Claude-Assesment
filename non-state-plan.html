The Non-State Search

The state exams are filed and eligibility sits banked for twelve months. This is everything else — and how to run it so it doesn't quietly collapse back into $22/hr help desk.

11 Aug 2026 · Sacramento region · six lanes

Why the tier rule gets harder here

State civil service makes the tier explicit. There are published classifications, and a B.S. in Computer Science literally satisfies the minimum qualification for IT Associate. You meet it or you don't. The degree does visible, mechanical work.

Nowhere else works like that. A private company has no "IT Associate" classification. So the rule has to be restated:

The replacement rule

Your degree is worth the most where the employer is most bureaucratic.

Employer typeDegree valueWhy
County · city · district · utility · transit Highest Published classifications, degree-based minimum qualifications, scored supplementals — the same machinery as the state
Health systems · universities · large manufacturers High Formal Analyst I/II/III ladders. "Bachelor's in CS or related" is a literal screen you pass and most applicants don't
Mid-size private with a real IT department Moderate No ladder, but a degree plus production-floor background is an unusual combination
Small business · MSP · generic staffing req Lowest Scored on years of experience alone. Where you lose to a six-year veteran at $24/hr

So weight the search toward structured employers. That is what "apply at your tier" means once you leave CalCareers.

The failure mode is the bottom row. Generic MSP and staffing help-desk reqs are the easiest to find, the fastest to apply to, and the only ones where your degree counts for nothing. Volume there feels like progress.

The six lanes

01

Local public sector

The closest thing to state service, and the most under-used option you have.

Titles
IT Analyst I/II · IT Infrastructure Analyst · IT Applications Analyst · Systems Support Specialist · IT Technician II/III · Client Services Analyst
Employers
Sacramento County (in flight) · City of Sacramento · Placer · Yolo · El Dorado · SMUD · SacRT · Regional San · Sac Metro Fire
Where
GovernmentJobs.com covers most of them on one account. Set the alerts once.
Why it works
Identical degree-qualification mechanism to the state, a thinner applicant pool than private, single-site, and the driving requirement usually doesn't apply.
Watch
Hard cut-off dates. Supplemental questionnaires are scored against the bulletin — write them in classification language, not résumé language.
02

Health systems

The best long game on this page.

Titles
Desktop Support Analyst · IT Service Desk Analyst II · Endpoint Support Analyst · Clinical Systems Support · Applications Analyst I
Employers
UC Davis Health · Sutter · Kaiser · Dignity/CommonSpirit · Marshall Medical
Why it works
Health systems carry an enormous internal analyst tier. The documented path is to get in at desktop support on one campus, then transfer internally — and internal transfer converts at 32× the inbound application rate, the strongest channel in the whole research file. Getting inside is the move; the entry title barely matters.
Watch
⚠ No verified Sacramento band was confirmed for entry desktop roles — check before assuming. UC Davis Health is UC, not state civil service: separate portal, separate rules, unrelated to your CalCareers eligibility.
03

Education, non-state

Titles

Employers
Los Rios CCD · FCUSD · Twin Rivers · EGUSD · San Juan · SCUSD · Natomas USD · SCOE

04

Industrial, food-ag, manufacturing

Your proven converter. The final two at Superior Farms came from this lane.

Titles
Manufacturing IT Support Technician · Plant IT Technician · IT/OT Technician · Site IT Technician · Production Systems Technician · MES Technician
Employers
Blue Diamond Growers · Nippon Shokken · Pacific Coast Producers · Sunsweet · Raley's · Nugget · UNFI · Sysco — plus the California League of Food Producers member list
Why it works
"Manufacturing experience preferred" repels the entire bootcamp and CS-grad pool before you're compared to them. Your cold-storage and food-safety background is a scored asset here and worth nothing anywhere else.
Watch
Mostly company-direct and unindexed — these don't reliably reach Indeed. Go to careers pages. Verify the commute before you invest.
05

Distribution and logistics

Hires continuously, and shift work is normal.

Titles
IT Support Technician II · Deskside Support · Warehouse Systems Support · FC IT Tech
Employers
Amazon · UPS · FedEx · Walmart DC · Target DC · UNFI
Why it works
Your throughput and deadline record reads as directly relevant rather than incidental. Amazon Tech II sits around $31/hr.
Watch
The Tier 1 version of these roles pays near $25/hr — push for the Tech II classification. Check transit.
06

Contract-to-hire agencies

Your fastest lane — and the one where this whole plan is most likely to break.

Firms
Robert Half (live relationship) · Insight Global · Randstad · TEKsystems · Apex Systems · Motion Recruitment
Why it works
Roughly 35% of temps convert to permanent, against ~0.5% of cold applications becoming hires. It's your demonstrated channel — Natomas came from here.
Watch
⚠ This is exactly where tier discipline breaks. A recruiter routes you on the band you state. Say "IT support" and you land in the $22–24/hr queue. Say the band out loud, every time, to every firm.

The ten-second test

Run this on a posting before spending any time on it.

Apply if any of these

  • A level in the title — I/II/III, Senior, Tech II. A level means a real ladder and a real band.
  • "Bachelor's degree required or preferred." The degree is doing work; most applicants don't have one.
  • Analyst · Administrator · Specialist · Engineer in the title, not just "Technician."
  • A domain qualifier — manufacturing, plant, production, clinical, campus, endpoint, systems.
  • A posted pay floor of $28/hr or better.

Skip if

  • "Help Desk Technician" or "Tier 1" with no level and no domain.
  • Pay floor under $25/hr — below the Sacramento median for this work.
  • "3–5 years required" with no degree-substitution clause.
  • Requires a driver's licence, personal vehicle, or travel between sites — until the licence lands.
  • A staffing req with no named client and no band.
The habit that's hardest to build

An easy application is not a cheap application. It costs the same attention as a good one and returns almost nothing.

What to say

One sentence — for recruiters, hiring managers, and any free-text field:

CS degree, ITIL 4 Foundation, A+ Core 1, with hands-on enterprise device support — Windows and macOS imaging, deployment and break-fix at district scale — plus production-floor and cold-storage operations experience. I'm looking at Tier 2/3 systems and analyst-track roles, single site, $30+/hr, open to contract or direct.

Three things it does deliberately: leads with the credential, names the band, and puts systems in the recruiter's ear rather than support.

On the gap Brooke named. "More experience with networking and systems" points at a specific bridge. Don't chase pure network engineering — that's a longer road. Chase the systems half: endpoint administration, device management, imaging and deployment at fleet scale, Jamf and Intune. It's adjacent to what you already do, it's a real tier above technician, and it's the shortest honest path from where you are to where the postings are.

Tracking, and what's still unknown

Every application goes in the tracker — including the fast ones. Three columns carry this plan:

ColumnWhy it matters
laneWhich of the six. Count them at month end — if lane 6 dominates and lanes 1–2 are empty, the plan has quietly reverted to the old search
next_actionAn application with no next action is a lottery ticket
contactA human name. Applications with one convert at a different rate than applications without one

Review monthly, not weekly. Three data points is noise. At thirty days the question is which lanes produced a human response — not which produced the most submissions.

⚠ No verified pay band for entry desktop roles at Sacramento health systems. Worth twenty minutes on the UCDH, Sutter and Kaiser careers pages before committing effort to lane 2.

⚠ Commute reality for lanes 4 and 5 stays unresolved until the licence lands. Several of the best industrial employers sit in Dixon, West Sacramento and Woodland.

The state lane runs 2–4 months, which makes these lanes the fast path, not the backup. If a lane 6 contract lands first, take it — it costs you nothing, and the exam eligibility sits banked for twelve months regardless.
